# Evolution Sim
## a remake of an old python project, implemented in C#.

The aim of this project is to simulate a food chain. The bunnies aim to eat food so they become satiated enough to reproduce. When they do, they have a child which inherits their parent's genes and adds a little mutation.

**Controls:**

- A to spawn more animals
- F to spawn more food

- Click on something to see information

- Use left and right arrow keys to fastforward/slowdown time

- Hold right click and drag to pan camera, scroll to zoom in/out
